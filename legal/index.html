Privacy

When you send us a request through a form on this site, the details you enter are delivered to us by email through a form service and used only to reply to you. We do not sell or share them. The forms are protected by Google reCAPTCHA, which is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. To have your details removed, email hello@northlakeboathouse.ca.
