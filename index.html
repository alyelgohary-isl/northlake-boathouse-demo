The approach

Seen first from the water.

A boathouse is the first thing you see from the lake, so we design it from the water, not the road.

The material

Cedar that belongs on the shore.

We build in cedar because it weathers the way the shoreline does, silvering slowly into the granite and pine around it.

Inside the slip

Room for the boat, and the long evenings.

Slips sized for your boats, and a deck or screened room above for evenings on the water.

The waterfront

Dock, shore and boathouse, one piece.

We plan the dock, cribs and stair with the building, so the waterfront reads as one.

Building on lakes since 1998

Built by the family that will still be here next summer.

Northlake has been building on Lake Muskoka and Lake Joseph since 1998. We design each boathouse and dock for its own shoreline, then build it with our own crew, so the people you meet at the first site visit are the people who hand you the keys.

There is no catalogue. Every project starts with your shore, your boat and how your family uses the water, and ends with something that looks like it has always been there.

Cedar, granite, water. Nothing that doesn't belong.
