From first email to launch day.

One crew and one point of contact from the first site visit to the day the boat goes in. Here is how a project runs.

Request a quote

  1. Tell us about your shore

    An email with a few photos, the boats you keep and roughly when you would like to be on the water.

  2. Site visit

    We walk the shoreline with you, check depths and exposure, and talk through what will and won't work on that granite.

  3. Design and approvals

    Drawings for your shore, revised until they are right, and we walk you through the approvals building over water needs.

  4. The build

    Our own crew, cedar we have chosen ourselves, and a schedule set around the water levels and the season.

  5. Launch day

    The boat goes in, the lanterns come on, and we hand you the keys. Then we are still here next summer.

Questions we get first

When should I get in touch?
As early as you like. Most boathouses are planned the season before they are built, so a first email in late summer or fall gives the most room for design, approvals and a spring start.
Do you work on both lakes?
Yes. We have built on Lake Muskoka and Lake Joseph since 1998, and those two lakes are where we work.
Do you build docks without a boathouse?
Yes. Many projects are a dock on its own, new or replacing one that has seen too many winters.
