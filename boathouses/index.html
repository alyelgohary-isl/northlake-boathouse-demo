What we design for

Four things decide how a boathouse lives on its shore. We settle them with you before anything is drawn.

The slip

Sized for the boats you keep now and the one you might keep later, with a walkway on both sides and the water depth checked at low levels.

The upper level

A sleeping loft, a screened room, a deck over the water or nothing at all. What goes up top depends on how your family spends its evenings.

The cedar

Shingle, board-and-batten or both. Cedar weathers the way the shoreline does, and we detail it so it does that evenly.

The shoreline

Granite, exposure to wind and ice, and where the sun sets all shape the plan. So do the rules for building over the water.
