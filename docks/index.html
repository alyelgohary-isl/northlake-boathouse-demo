Docks built for the water they sit in.

Cribs, floats or a mix; a straight run or a wraparound with room for chairs. We build docks the way we build boathouses: in cedar, for the shoreline they are on.

What we plan for

A dock that is still level and square in ten years was planned for the water, not just the view.

Water levels

The lakes rise and fall through the season. We set the deck height for the year's range, not the week we visit.

Ice and wind

Cribs on exposed shores, floats where the ice moves, and a removable section where the winter asks for one.

The boats

Where you tie up, which side the wind pushes you onto, and how deep it is at the bow in August.

The mornings

Room at the end for two chairs and a coffee. It is the part of the dock people use most, so we don't treat it as leftover.
