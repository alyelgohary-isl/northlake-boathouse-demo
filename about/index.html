Request a quoteHow we work

The people at the site visit are the people who hand you the keys.

Northlake is a family business. We design each boathouse and dock ourselves, build it with our own crew, and answer our own email. There is no sales office between you and the people doing the work.

That is also why there is no catalogue. Every shoreline on these lakes is different, and a boathouse that looks right on a Lake Joseph point would look wrong in a Lake Muskoka bay. We draw for the shore in front of us.

Cedar, granite, water. Patience.
